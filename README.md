# Roast the Smart Fridge

Entry for the make.fun competition

## The entry

> My smart fridge charges a monthly subscription to keep food cold, so I now pay rent to a box that does the one job fridges did for free in 1985.

144 characters, one line, one sentence, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

- **On subject:** a roast of the smart fridge itself, in the first person — the writer lives with it and pays for it.
- **Fresh angle:** not the stock joke about a fridge ordering too much milk. The target is the subscription creep: a smart fridge paywalling the one job every dumb fridge did for free.
- **Concrete and specific:** a monthly subscription to keep food cold — a real smart-appliance behaviour, not a vague joke about smart homes in general.
- **Surprise turn:** the punchline reframes the appliance as a landlord — "pay rent to a box" — and the sting lands on a year, 1985, when fridges just… refrigerated.
- **Clean voice:** sets up fast, lands in one breath, no padding, no second sentence.
